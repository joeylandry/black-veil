# The Black Veil: objectives draft (v1, for your veto)

Built from the photos of your house, the Wilson call, and every earlier session. Nothing here is on the site. Every item and pairing is a suggestion: cross out what you don't like and I'll redo it.

Each guest gets five objectives, the same shape for everyone:

1. **FIND**: a hidden house object (read the tag on it).
2. **SWAP**: a mirrored trade with one named partner.
3. **LEARN**: a small secret that two named guests know (secrets are still to be written, "coming soon" on the cards).
4. **MONEY or SOCIAL**: something to do with the fake money, or with another guest.
5. **STORY**: a clue about the 1924 story, a role job, or a secret affair.

Survivors swap three of these for their own chain (see the end).

---

## 1. What's new from the Wilson call
- Survivors **know the old man tried to kill them and their friends**, and they know who he is.
- They know they survived and that other survivors probably exist, but **not how many or who**.
- They don't know whether Cassie is alive. They may think the old man killed her.
- Everyone's character card says the survivors were there.
- There are two killers in the story: the old man (1924) and the survivor who kills him tonight.
- At the end: "I present to you the new head of the Black Veil" (the killer is crowned).

### Two problems this creates (need your call)
1. **Cassie's invitation.** Survivors got invitations with their fake names on them, so someone who knew those names wrote them. But they don't know Cassie lives. Suggested fix: the invitation is **unsigned**, and working out that only Cassie knew their fake names is how a survivor discovers she's alive. That can be one of their objectives.
2. **Survivors already know a lot of 1924.** If they know the old man hunted them, they have a head start on the old mystery and the connection question. Options: (a) they know only their own survival (the what), not the Exit plan, the fake receipts or Cassie; (b) they get replacement questions for the parts they know, like the killer and the Butler; (c) a flat discount on those points. I'd start with (a).
