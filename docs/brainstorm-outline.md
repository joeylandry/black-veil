# Black Veil: brainstorm outline

Source: Joey's voice transcripts with a friend (Oct 19) and follow-up messages (Oct 19-21). Where something was never said, it is listed under Open, not filled in.

## Locked

**The night**
- Victim is the old man, a fictional stuffed dummy (a real guest can't die and be out of the game). He is revealed at about 9:00, not present on arrival.
- Mingling and objectives until the reveal, then investigation. At the end: submit final answers, one group wrap-up, music flips to 2026 (no time machine). Folk music all night, then it gets bumpy.
- No town hall or deliberation during the game. The game runs itself.
- Joe stages the murder (a glass by the dummy). Joe scans the house for props.

**The story**
- Three main mysteries: the old murder, the new murder, and the connection. Side quests around them.
- 1924 (version A, "The Exit"): federal Prohibition pressure is closing in. Cassie tells her inner circle a raid is coming and "they were all going to get cooked", so they agreed to her staged massacre: everyone is "dead" to the law and rivals, takes a new name and a payout from the vault.
- The old man is the Keeper, the bookkeeper who runs the vault. After the staged massacre he ambushes Cassie, ousts her, takes the vault and the books, and hires a hitman so nobody is left to say the money existed.
- It looks like Cassie did it: the hit orders are backed by **fake receipts** in Cassie's name. People are up to no good, but the connection must be clear and simple (the ledger/blackmail idea stays minimal).
- Cassie is alive, wounded in the ambush, hiding. She paid for the five survivors' new identities in 1924. **Real receipts** prove that (contrast with the fake ones).
- Five survivors escaped the hitman, killed him, and came back under fake identities.
- Invitations have two senders: the old man invites the 19 regular guests; Cassie secretly sends each survivor a separate invitation. Cassie at least alerts them. Each survivor's invitation carries something unique to them (for example a numbered token).
- Survivors compete: it is a succession. Whoever finishes the old man takes Cassie's seat and the club (the Veil). That satisfies Cassie. Everyone also has a private personal motive depending on their character.
- Survivors are spread across the persona groups, one in each.
- The poison: one bottle, held by the Butler (Rebecca). Whoever possesses the bottle is the one who knows who got it first.

**Characters and cards**
- Characters are based on real guest names; Joel is J. Bruce Wayne.
- Cards have public info (shareable) and private info (not).
- A short printed card with bullets of what is known about 1924, also on the website.

**Objectives and points**
- About five objectives each: find an object, money, swap an item, a secret lover (hide or seek), learn from others.
- Proof is typed in; Claude judges free text at the end. Resubmit freely, last answer counts, no feedback until the end, no live leaderboard.
- Points accumulate in tiers. Within a group of five everyone can earn the same objective maximum; the killer is one point above. The three mysteries lift you above the other groups. Someone who finishes every errand but never solves the story must not win.
- Everyone may name up to five survivors: right earns, wrong loses.
