The wall around Cassie: how to hang it

Print every page on cream or light-tan paper at 100% (not "fit to page"). Staining a page with weak tea and drying it flat makes it look 1924. Cut along the dotted lines where there are any.

Hang these in plain sight, around the portrait

  • Page 2: the Farrago front page. Biggest piece. Pin it to the wall beside her.
  • Page 3: the police bulletin, and three closure notices. Hang the bulletin by the Farrago. Put the three closure notices in three different places in the house (the site says the notice was posted in three places).
  • Page 4: three receipts. Pin them next to her face. These are the fakes: they make Cassie look guilty.
  • Page 5: the admission card, vigil card, portrait plate and note. Cut them out. Plate goes on the frame, vigil card on the table in front, admission card next to the candle. Tuck the note into the corner of the frame so just an edge shows.
  • Page 6: torn page from the 1923 books. Tape it to the wall low, off to one side.

Hide this one

  • The real receipt is in its own file (real-receipt-under-portrait.pdf). Tape it to the back of the portrait, or under it. Do not hang it on the wall.

Do not

  • Put any filing codes, names of survivors, or the word "Keeper" on the wall. The pages are already written without them.

The Evening Farrago

Manchester, N.H. · Saturday, 1 November 1924 · Two Cents

TERROR AT THE BLACK VEIL
“Massacre,” Say Guests

MANCHESTER. Guests who fled the Black Veil supper club on Elm Street late last night described gunfire in the dark and blood on the floor.

No coroner was called. No ambulance came. No undertaker was summoned. No body has been found and no name has been given.

The proprietress, Miss Castello, cannot be found.
