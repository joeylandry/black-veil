MEMORANDUM

THE VAULT

It opens to three keys turned together, and then a dial.

One key, the Keeper's ring. One key, the Silent Partner's brass key. One key, C.C.'s diamond bar pin.

The dial is set, and known, by C.C. alone.

“I have two keys and the pin's maker. I have the lock. I do not have the lady.”

THE EVENING FARRAGO

1 November 1924Manchester, N. H.Extra
TERROR AT THE BLACK VEIL: “MASSACRE,” SAY GUESTS

Fled guests described gunfire and blood on the floor. No coroner was called. No ambulance came. No undertaker was summoned. No body has been found and no name has been given.

The proprietress, Miss Castello, cannot be found.

COUNTY PHYSICIAN

preliminary examination
Deceasedan old man, white hair and beard; pocket watch engraved “K.”
Causepoisoning. Strychnine, as nux vomica, taken in rye whiskey. The glass was sweetened with honey to hide the bitterness.
Timethe dose was taken at about nine o'clock; death within the quarter hour.
Bottlean apothecary's vial from a house stock, not a druggist's pack.
