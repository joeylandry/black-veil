## 1. The story in plain words

**1924.** Cassie Castello runs the Black Veil, a supper club above the Merrimack, opened December 1921. Kit Kessler, the Keeper, keeps its books. The Mahns family are the silent partners. In the autumn of 1924 federal pressure is closing in, so Cassie plans the **Exit**: a staged massacre on All Hallows' Eve, so the Veil's twenty-two people can vanish with new names and a payout from the vault. Adam sells her the blanks, Luke builds the stage blood.

At 11:02 four go up to the private landing (Cassie, Kit, the Silent Partner, the Notary) and the Exit is witnessed. At 11:30 the "massacre" happens and the guests flee to the papers. At 11:47 the music stops, Kit signs out, and he leaves with Cassie by the river door. At the launch he **ambushes her**, takes the books, and she escapes wounded to Rebecca's boarding house (Room 3). Kit pays Rebecca $200 to forget her.

Kit tells the scattered members that Cassie is finished. On 4 November he prepares **the List** of the twenty-two, and on the 8th he hires a hitman, Quill, paying him out of the Teller's own share. Between December 1924 and September 1925 seventeen of them die in "accidents." Three orders "in Cassie's hand" are shown to a court, and receipts in her name come from Kit's bank: all forged by the Notary under threat, in a hand that rings its i's. **Five could not be found.** On 30 September 1925 Quill reports all twenty-two settled but cannot show five. In October 1925 the five find Quill first and kill him, and they go under new names. Cassie, hiding in Montreal, paid for their escape. Her Christmas card comes that December.

**1926.** On 22 October Kit returns to Manchester and asks the Butler for the Merrimack Room and a rye at nine o'clock. **He has sent invitations to the 19 regular guests**, because he holds a secret on every one of them. Cassie, from Montreal, has sent each of **the five survivors** a separate, unsigned invitation with a numbered marker. They know they have to end him. Why is theirs to learn.

**The Succession, his plan.** At half past nine, after his nightcap, Kit means to crown himself the new head of the Black Veil before the 19 as witnesses (the black satin cloth is the Veil). He needs them to do it: the Sheriff swears him in, the Judge signs the charter, the Doctor certifies that Cassie is dead, the Lawyer files it, the Newsie and the radio announcer print and broadcast it, the Butler hands over the House. Their secrets are the leash. What he still lacks is Cassie's pin and the dial for the vault. He does not know the five are in the room.

**The night.** The House sells its single vial to the first survivor with a marker. At nine the nightcap is served. At 9:15 the Butler finds the old man dead and screams. Whoever bought the vial is the killer and, at the end, is **crowned the new head of the Black Veil**.
