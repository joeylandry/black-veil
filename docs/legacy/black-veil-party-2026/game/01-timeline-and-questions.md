# Timeline

- **Dec 1921**: Cassandra “Cassie” Castello opens the Black Veil above the Merrimack. Kit Kessler keeps its books. The Mahns family is the Silent Partner.
- **1922–23**: The Amoskeag strike, Canadian liquor on the river, protection by the quarter. The Veil is where it all gets settled.
- **Oct 1924**: Federal pressure builds. Cassie plans the Exit: a staged massacre as cover, new names and a vault payout for twenty-two members.
- **31 Oct 1924, 23:02**: A party of four goes up to the private landing: C.C., K.K., J.M. (the Silent Partner) and E.L. (the Notary). The Exit Compact is witnessed.
- **31 Oct 1924, 23:30**: Stage blood, breakaway furniture and blanks make a massacre. The guests flee, the police come, and they report it.
- **31 Oct 1924, 23:47**: The music stops. Kit signs out of the register. At the launch Kit ambushes Cassie and takes the books; she escapes, wounded, to Rebecca Chow's boarding house, and Kit pays Rebecca $200 to forget Room 3.
- **After**: Kit takes over the Veil and hires hitmen against the twenty-two. Seventeen are settled in “accidents,” and three orders in Cassie's hand are shown to a court; they are forged by the Notary under duress. Five survive. Kit never learns it.
- **Cassie**: Hiding, and believed dead. She knows the five lived.
- **Oct 1926**: Nineteen black envelopes signed “The House” go out, and five unsigned ones from Cassie.
- **22 Oct 1926**: Kit Kessler returns to Manchester and asks the Butler for the Merrimack Room and a rye at nine o'clock.
- **23 Oct 1926, 20:00**: The party. Five survivors under fake identities compete for the House's single vial.
- **23 Oct 1926, 21:00**: The nightcap is served. The House is closed.
- **23 Oct 1926, 21:15**: The Butler finds the Keeper dead, and screams.
- **23 Oct 1926, 21:30**: The hour Kit had set to crown himself head of the Black Veil.

## The nine final questions

- **old-1** (1): What really happened at the Black Veil on All Hallows' Eve, 1924?
- **old-2** (1): Why did the Veil's members all leave with Cassie that night?
- **old-3** (1): Who killed the members afterward, and how did it look like someone else did it?
- **new-1** (2): Who poisoned the Keeper?
- **new-2** (1): How was he poisoned, and where did the poison come from?
- **new-3** (1): Why did the killer do it? What had the Keeper done to them?
- **con-1** (1): Who was the dead man to the Veil, and what had he done?
- **con-2** (1): How do the five survivors connect to him?
- **con-3** (1): Who sent the invitations, and why?
- **alt-1** (2): What did the Keeper come to the Black Veil to do tonight, and how did he mean to do it?
- **alt-2** (1): Where are the Keeper's books, and what does it take to open them?
