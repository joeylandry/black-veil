# Timeline

- **Dec 1921**: Cassandra “Cassie” Castello opens the Black Veil above the Merrimack. Kit Kessler keeps its books. The Mahns family is the Silent Partner.
- **1922–23**: The Amoskeag strike, Canadian liquor on the river, protection by the quarter. The Veil is where it all gets settled.
- **Oct 1924**: Federal pressure builds. Cassie plans the Exit: a staged massacre as cover, new names and a vault payout for twenty-two members.
- **31 Oct 1924, 22:10**: The orchestra is admitted at the river door.
- **31 Oct 1924, 23:02**: A party of four goes up to the private landing: C.C., K.K., J.M. (the Silent Partner) and E.L. (the Notary). The Exit Compact is witnessed.
- **31 Oct 1924, 23:30**: Stage blood, breakaway furniture and blanks make a massacre. Guests flee and tell the papers.
- **31 Oct 1924, 23:47**: The music stops. Kit signs out of the register. Cassie and Kit leave by the river door. At the launch Kit ambushes her; she escapes, wounded, to Rebecca Chou's boarding house.
- **1 Nov 1924**: Kit tells the scattered members that Cassie is finished and places each of them somewhere to lie quiet. He pays Rebecca $200 to forget Room 3.
- **4 Nov 1924**: Kit prepares the List of twenty-two. A. Quill is retained on the 8th, paid from the Teller's own share.
- **Dec 1924 – Sep 1925**: Seventeen members are settled in “accidents.” Three orders in Cassie's hand are shown to a court; they are forged by the Notary under duress.
- **3 Mar 1925**: The Wren's aeroplane falls into Lake Massabesic. The Wren survives; Cal Yates does not.
- **30 Sep 1925**: Quill reports all twenty-two settled; he cannot show five. Kit does not believe him.
- **Oct 1925**: The survivors find Quill first and kill him. Two memorial notices (Crane, Ballard) are placed in the Farrago and withdrawn. The survivors have gone under new names.
- **22 Dec 1925**: Cassie's Christmas card, postmarked Manchester.
- **7 Oct 1926**: Black envelopes appear in Elm Street and on the West Side. A Montreal wire asks WHO PREPARED THE LIST.
- **22 Oct 1926**: Kit Kessler returns to Manchester and asks the Butler for the Merrimack Room and a rye at nine o'clock.
- **23 Oct 1926, 20:00**: The party. Five survivors under fake identities compete for the House's single vial.
- **23 Oct 1926, 21:00**: The nightcap is served. The House is closed.
- **23 Oct 1926, 21:15**: The Butler finds the Keeper dead, and screams.

## The nine final questions

- **old-1** (10): What really happened at the Black Veil on All Hallows' Eve, 1924?
- **old-2** (10): Why did the Veil's members all leave with Cassie that night?
- **old-3** (10): Who killed the members afterward, and how did it look like someone else did it?
- **new-1** (16): Who poisoned the Keeper?
- **new-2** (12): How was he poisoned, and where did the poison come from?
- **new-3** (12): Why did the killer do it? What had the Keeper done to them?
- **con-1** (10): Who was the dead man to the Veil, and what had he done?
- **con-2** (10): How do the five survivors connect to him?
- **con-3** (10): Who sent the invitations, and why?
