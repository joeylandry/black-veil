# The Black Veil: simplified game (DRAFT v4)

*Proposal built from the host's notes. Nothing in the code or the generated docs has been changed yet. Spoilers.*

## Story

- Dec 1921: Cassie Castello opens the Black Veil. Kit Kessler keeps the books.
- 31 Oct 1924: The Exit, a staged massacre (stage blood, blanks, the Exit Compact witnessed). The guests flee and the police come.
- That night: Kit ambushes Cassie at the launch and takes the books. She escapes, wounded, to Rebecca's boarding house. Kit pays Rebecca $200 to forget her. Cassie goes into hiding; nobody knows she is alive.
- Kit takes over and hires hitmen against the 22 members. Seventeen die in "accidents", with orders in Cassie's hand (forged). Five survive. Kit does not know it.
- Oct 1926: Kit sends 19 invitations (he holds a secret on each guest). Cassie sends 5 to the survivors, each with a numbered marker. She knows they are alive. They know only that they survived and must end him; why is their own errand.
- 23 Oct 1926: nightcap at 9:00, a scream at 9:15, and at 9:30 Kit meant to crown himself head of the Black Veil. The Butler sells one vial, to the first marker. That survivor is the killer.

**Cut:** Quill, Cal Yates's plane, Pat Dunn, the memorial notices, the Christmas card, the Montreal wire, "Kit tells the members she is finished", most dates, the radio transmitter.

## Objective types, in build order

1. **Money.** Non-rich guests: leave with $60 more than you started with. Rich guests: pay out $100 for clues and still hold $300.
2. **Find.** A tagged object in a shared room: in, on, **behind or under** something (the globe, a lamp base, a drawer-front, the underside of a table). Read its code.
3. **Swap.** Go to the person, trade the item (or pay), read the code on what you get.
4. **Secret.** Ten small facts, each known by two people. A holder must **hide it from one named guest and tell it to the seeker**; the seeker enters the fact on the site to confirm.
5. **Role.** Only that character has it.

## Survivors' objectives (all five get the same set)

Find, then discover who you are, why you want him dead, and the hour; then act.

1. **Find** a tagged object.
2. **Who you were.** Learn your own real name and trade from the guests who can clear your tell.
3. **Why you.** Learn what the Keeper took from you.
4. **The old man's hour.** Learn when he takes his nightcap.
5. **Make your offer** to the Butler at the bar before 9:00 (a marker for the vial).

Mihir also starts rich and may pay for clues.
