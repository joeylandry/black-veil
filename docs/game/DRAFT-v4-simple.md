# The Black Veil: simplified game (DRAFT v4)

*A proposal built from the host's notes. Nothing in the code or the generated docs has been changed yet. Spoilers.*

## Story

- Dec 1921: Cassie Castello opens the Black Veil. Kit Kessler keeps the books.
- 31 Oct 1924: The Exit, a staged massacre (stage blood, blanks, the Exit Compact witnessed). The guests flee and the police come.
- That night: Kit ambushes Cassie at the launch and takes the books. She escapes, wounded, to Rebecca's boarding house. Kit pays Rebecca $200 to forget her. Cassie then goes into hiding. Nobody knows she is alive.
- Kit takes over, and hires hitmen against the 22 members. Seventeen die in "accidents", with orders in Cassie's hand (forged). Five survive. Kit does not know it.
- Oct 1926: Kit sends 19 invitations (he holds a secret on each guest). Cassie sends 5 to the survivors, with a numbered marker each. She knows they are alive. They know only that they survived and must end him. Why is their own errand.
- 23 Oct 1926: nightcap at 9:00, a scream at 9:15, and at 9:30 Kit meant to crown himself head of the Black Veil. The Butler sells one vial, to the first marker. That survivor is the killer.

**Cut:** Quill, Cal Yates's plane, Pat Dunn, the memorial notices, the Christmas card, the Montreal wire, "Kit tells the members she is finished", most dates.

## Objective types, in build order

Every guest has five objectives, one point each, built from these in this order:

1. **Money.** Guests pay, earn or keep cash. Some start rich.
2. **Hidden thing.** Find a tagged object in a shared room and read the code.
3. **Swap.** Go to the person, trade the item or pay, and read the code on what you receive.
4. **Secret.** A few people each hold one piece of knowledge. Holders must **hide it from one person and share it with another**. The one who receives it must **enter the fact on the site** to confirm. Small and local: two or three holders per secret.
5. **Role.** The objective only that character has (below).
