- **Walk, don't run**, and keep your drink in your hand when the excitement starts.
- Nothing is ever taken from anyone. If a prop moves, it's by a deal.
- Hidden game cards are only in shared spaces, never in bedrooms, bathrooms, closets or drawers, and each has a black seal sticker.
