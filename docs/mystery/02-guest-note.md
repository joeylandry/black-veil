## A. The group note (send to everyone)

**Subject: Your character is waiting. The Veil lifts Friday.**

You're on the list for **The Black Veil: a masquerade and a murder** on **Friday, October 23, doors at 8:00 PM**, 3 Warren Street, Manchester, N.H.

**Your character**
Sign in at the Guest Ledger with the email you RSVP'd with: **[SITE LINK]**. Your character's public card is waiting there. Your **secrets and private objectives unlock at 7:55 PM on Friday**, so you can't peek early, and nothing is lost by waiting.

**The game starts the moment you walk in.** Some of you will arrive carrying something, or watching for someone. Be on time: your arrival moves other people's stories.

**What to expect (the short version)**

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| **8:00 PM** | Doors open. Cocktails, introductions, and a secret mission for everyone |
| 8:00–8:45 | Mingle, make deals, keep your secrets |
| **8:45 PM** | A body is discovered |
| 8:45–9:45 | Investigate, interrogate, trade clues, betray someone |
| **9:45 PM** | Final accusations, and the Sheriff makes an arrest |
| **10:00 PM** | The grand reveal. The murderer is exposed and a winner is crowned |
| **10:00 PM onward** | **The Black Veil After Dark.** The mystery is over and the music jumps to **2026**. You can drop your character and dance, or **stay in character and act scandalized by the modern music**. Both are encouraged |

Everyone has a real chance to win, and **there is exactly one winner**. You earn points by doing your missions, meeting people, finding things and solving the case. Money in the game is for deals and bribes. It is **not** worth any points at the end.

**A few house rules**
- **Bring your phone, charged.** You'll use it to check in, log clues and file your final answer.
- **Walk, don't run**, and keep your drink in your hand when the excitement starts.
- Nothing is ever taken from anyone. If a prop moves, it's by a deal.
- Hidden game cards are only in shared spaces, never in bedrooms, bathrooms, closets or drawers, and each has a black seal sticker.
- The game is built to run itself. You don't need to wait for me for anything.

**Dress: 1920s formal and a mask**

Cocktails: **[COCKTAIL LIST]**

See you at the door.
